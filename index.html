About

Systems thinking for FMCG distribution

A grounding in trade marketing and sales administration, applied through dashboards, automation and route planning.

I work at the intersection of trade marketing and business analytics — building the promotional programs that move product through distributors and retailers, and the reporting systems that tell management whether they worked.

Over the past four years across Meghna Group of Industries, Square Toiletries and the Coca-Cola bottling network, I've managed national display programs spanning 15,000 outlets, led secondary sales automation rollouts across entire regions, and built the distributor and territory databases that field teams rely on daily.

I'm most useful in the space between the field and the spreadsheet: translating what distributors and retailers are telling sales reps into a Power BI dashboard, a trade program, or a coverage plan that a General Manager can act on.
